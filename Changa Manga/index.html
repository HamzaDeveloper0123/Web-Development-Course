History

Planted in 1866 by the British for railway timber, with a forestry museum and a mini train from that era.

A forest built by hand

Every tree in Changa Manga was planted by people. Started in 1866 to supply fuel wood for the North-Western Railway, it grew into one of the largest man-made forests in the world.

Today it is a wildlife park, a vulture breeding centre and a favourite picnic and camping spot for families across Punjab. Take the forest tram, row on the lake, walk the shaded trails, and end the day around a bonfire.

Good to know

When is the forest open?

Daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Night camping guests stay overnight at the lakeside campsite.

How far is it from Lahore?

About 80 km, so it works as a single day trip or a weekend stay.

Do I need to bring camping gear?

No. Tents and sleeping bags are provided, and binoculars come with the bird watching walk.
